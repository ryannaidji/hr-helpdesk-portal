SYNTHETIC: fictional policy of the fictional company Halcyon Ridge Logistics.

## Harassment and discrimination
Harassment and discrimination of any kind are prohibited. Reports can be made to any HR partner or through the confidential ethics line, and HR acknowledges each report within 2 working days.

## Non-retaliation
Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is prohibited and is itself grounds for dismissal.

## Conflicts of interest
Employees must disclose outside business interests to HR within 30 days of the interest arising.

## Gifts
Employees may not accept gifts worth more than 50 dollars from suppliers.
