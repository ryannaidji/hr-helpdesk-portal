SYNTHETIC: fictional policy of the fictional company Halcyon Ridge Logistics.

## Primary caregiver
Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave after 6 months of service.

## Secondary caregiver
Secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks of fully paid parental leave after 6 months of service. The leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

## Adoption and fostering
Adoptive parents and long-term foster parents have the same entitlement as birth parents.

## Return to work
Employees may return on a phased schedule at 60 percent of normal hours for up to 4 weeks at full pay. Employees return to the same role or an equivalent one.
