SYNTHETIC: fictional policy of the fictional company Halcyon Ridge Logistics.

## Submission deadline
Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense. Claims older than 90 days are not reimbursed.

## Meals
The meal limit during business travel is 65 dollars per day. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## Air travel
Economy class is required for flights under 8 hours. Premium economy is allowed on flights of 8 hours or longer.

## Mileage
Use of a personal car for business is reimbursed at 0.58 dollars per mile. Commuting is not reimbursable.
