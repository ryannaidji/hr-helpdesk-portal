SYNTHETIC: fictional policy of the fictional company Halcyon Ridge Logistics.

## Entitlement
Employees receive 12 paid sick days per calendar year. Unused sick days do not carry over.

## Notification
Employees must notify their manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence. The reason for the absence does not need to be shared with the manager.

## Medical certificate
A medical certificate is required for sick absences of 4 consecutive working days or more. Certificates are sent only to HR and are stored separately from the personnel file.

## Long-term illness
After 20 consecutive sick days, HR opens a return-to-work plan with the employee. Pay continues at 70 percent of base salary from day 21 up to day 90.
