SYNTHETIC: fictional policy of the fictional company Halcyon Ridge Logistics.

## Health insurance
Health coverage starts on the first day of the month after the hire date. The company pays 85 percent of the employee premium and 60 percent of the dependent premium.

## Retirement plan
The company matches retirement contributions up to 5 percent of base salary. Matched funds vest fully after 3 years of service.

## Learning budget
Each employee has a learning budget of 1200 dollars per calendar year. Unused budget does not roll over.

## Open enrollment
Open enrollment runs from November 1 to November 15. Changes outside this window need a qualifying life event reported within 30 days.
