SYNTHETIC: fictional policy of the fictional company Halcyon Ridge Logistics.

## Annual leave
Full-time employees accrue 22 days of annual leave per calendar year, credited monthly. Part-time employees accrue annual leave pro rata to their contracted hours.

## Carryover
Up to 5 unused annual leave days may be carried into the next calendar year. Carried-over days expire on March 31. Days above the carryover limit are forfeited on December 31.

## Requesting leave
Leave requests are submitted in the portal at least 10 working days before the first day of leave for absences longer than 3 days. Shorter absences need at least 2 working days of notice. A single request can cover at most 60 working days.

## Approval
The direct manager approves annual leave of 10 working days or fewer. HR approves annual leave longer than 10 working days and all sick, parental and unpaid leave requests. Nobody may approve their own request. Balance is deducted only once a request is approved.

## Unpaid leave
Employees with at least 12 months of service may request up to 30 working days of unpaid leave per calendar year. Benefits continue during unpaid leave.
