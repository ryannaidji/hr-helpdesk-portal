SYNTHETIC: fictional policy of the fictional company Halcyon Ridge Logistics.

## Eligibility
Employees in office-based roles may work remotely up to 3 days per week after completing probation. Warehouse and fleet roles are not eligible.

## Working from abroad
Working from another country is allowed for at most 20 working days per calendar year and needs written HR approval at least 15 working days ahead.

## Equipment
The company provides a laptop and a one-time home office allowance of 400 dollars. Receipts must be filed within 60 days of purchase.

## Core hours
Remote employees must be reachable during core hours, 10:00 to 15:00 in their local time zone.
